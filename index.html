How to use the LoveGoBuy spreadsheet

  1. Record the exact option. Save the product URL, color and size. For shoes, check whether the measurement refers to foot length or insole length. For clothing, compare garment width and length with an item you own.
  2. Check the buying options. Open the CAFANS listing and select LoveGoBuy if it appears under Buy Link. Confirm the item and selected variant on the agent’s order page.
  3. Request the shipping estimate. Use your destination and ask which weight the shipping line charges for. Keep domestic shipping, international shipping and service fees in separate columns.
  4. Review warehouse photos if available. Compare the variant, measurements and visible condition with your order before approving shipment. Check the exchange window before deciding.

Compare the full cost. Add the item price, domestic shipping, agent fees, international shipping and any applicable taxes or duties. Compare quotes in the same currency using rates from the same day.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need Excel to use the directory?

You can browse the products and filter the list in your browser. The CSV opens in Excel, Google Sheets or another spreadsheet app.

Where do the listings and prices come from?

The displayed items and estimated USD prices come from the CAFANS featured product directory. Open the product listing and check your agent’s current quote before ordering.

Where can I check quality-control photos?

Ask the agent whether warehouse inspection photos are available for the order. Use photos of your exact size and color to review the item before shipment.
